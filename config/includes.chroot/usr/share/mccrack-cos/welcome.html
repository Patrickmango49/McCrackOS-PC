Welcome to
McCrack-COS.

A desktop experience built on Debian and KDE Plasma, with a dark interface and cyan details. This is an early live-build preview.

Find your apps

Open the application launcher to find Firefox, Dolphin, Konsole, System Settings, and the included KDE tools.

Get connected

Use the network icon in the panel to connect to Wi-Fi or a wired network. Network availability depends on your hardware and firmware.

Explore McCrack

McCrackify and McCrackTube are available in the application menu. They open the web versions in Firefox and require an internet connection.

Personalize your desktop

Open System Settings to adjust display, sound, appearance, keyboard, and other desktop preferences.

Preview build: This image is intended for testing in a virtual machine. It is not an installer release. A live session may not preserve changes after shutdown.
